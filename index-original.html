Before you choose.

What is a Bluia FanPage?

A FanPage is a dedicated space for photos, videos and themed albums featuring your chosen Bluia. Bluias are AI-created visual models, and your personal FanPage is prepared for you.

Can I see a FanPage before subscribing?

Yes. Explore Kiara’s free FanPage preview to see the format, images, videos and albums before choosing a model.

Does one subscription cover every model?

Each personal FanPage has its own $10 USD monthly subscription. Choose the model you want when requesting your FanPage.

How do I receive my subscription URL?

After payment, we’ll prepare your personal FanPage and send its subscription URL to the email address you provide during payment.
